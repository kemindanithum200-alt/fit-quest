Rest and normal daily movement.

🍽️ Fuel guide

BREAKFAST
Eggs + whole-grain bread + fruit, or oatmeal + milk/yogurt + banana.
LUNCH
Protein + rice/pasta/potatoes/bread + vegetables + fruit.
SNACK
Fruit + yogurt, milk + banana, sandwich, or nuts + fruit.
DINNER
Protein + a carbohydrate + vegetables.

No calorie counting or intentional food restriction. At your age, regular balanced meals help provide energy for school, activity and normal growth.

😴 Sleep tracker

Aim for 8–10 hours each night.

📏 Growth log

Optional: record your height occasionally, under similar conditions. Look at changes over months, not day-to-day.

There is no exercise or food that can guarantee extra height. Genetics and puberty are the biggest factors; sleep, nutrition and activity help support normal development.

⚡ Quest rules

Start easy • Learn technique • Increase difficulty gradually • Rest when needed • Stop if you feel sharp pain, dizzy or unwell • Focus on being stronger and healthier rather than chasing an anime body.
