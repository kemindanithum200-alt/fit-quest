🥗 Balanced Food Guide

Breakfast
Oats/cereal + milk or yogurt + fruit
Lunch
Rice/pasta/bread + vegetables + a protein food

No calorie counting or intentional food restriction. Your body needs enough food and nutrients while you're growing.

⚡ Your Rules
